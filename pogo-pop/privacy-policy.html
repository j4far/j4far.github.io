Pogo Pop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Pogo Pop ("the game", "we") is a casual puzzle game. This policy explains what data is handled when you play.

Data we collect

We do not run any servers and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters and settings) is saved only on your own device and is deleted if you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about ad interactions. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Children

Pogo Pop is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
